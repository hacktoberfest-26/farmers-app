About location & installation

Allow precise location in your phone settings. Outdoors usually gives a better fix. The ring shows reported GPS uncertainty; indoor or room-level accuracy is not guaranteed. Tracking works while this app is open.

Install from your browser menu, or on iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen. The app shell works offline after the first visit; map tiles need a connection. This is an independent prototype, not an official H-FARM app.
